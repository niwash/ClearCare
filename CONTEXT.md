# ClearCare

A public record of what HIQA inspectors found in each designated centre, what the registered provider committed to do about it, and what later inspections said. Every statement is the regulator's or the provider's own published words, with its date and page.

## Language

### Centres and providers

**Centre**:
A designated centre registered with HIQA, identified by its HIQA centre ID. A nursing home is a centre for older people; the same term covers centres for people with disabilities.
_Avoid_: home, nursing home, facility

**Centre record**:
The timeline of every public event about one centre.
_Avoid_: home record, profile

**Registered provider**:
The legal entity registered with HIQA to run a centre, identified by its company registration (CRO) number. Short form: provider.
_Avoid_: operator, owner, chain, company

**Provider group**:
A set of providers under common control, where each membership has a date range and a published source. Never inferred from similar names.
_Avoid_: chain, brand

**Register snapshot**:
A whole HIQA register (the register of centres, or the Section 64 register) as downloaded on one day.
_Avoid_: register dump, register export

**Register change**:
A difference in one centre's register entry between two consecutive register snapshots, such as a new provider, name, capacity, condition or registration date, or the centre appearing on or leaving the register. Dated by the snapshot in which it was first seen, never as the day it happened.
_Avoid_: register event, update

**Condition of registration**:
A condition attached to a centre's registration, in HIQA's words. Conditions 1 to 3 are standard for every centre; later conditions are specific to one centre. HIQA's own term.
_Avoid_: registration condition, restriction, sanction

### Enforcement

**Enforcement event**:
A regulatory action that HIQA states, in a publication naming the centre, it took against the centre or its provider: a court application or cancellation, an urgent compliance plan, entry on the Section 64 register, or a published compliance notice. Dated as its source dates it. A condition of registration is not an enforcement event unless HIQA says so.
_Avoid_: sanction, penalty, enforcement action

**Section 64 register**:
HIQA's list of centres taken over by the HSE after their registration was cancelled.
_Avoid_: HSE-run list

### Inspections and findings

**Inspection**:
One HIQA inspection of one centre, identified by HIQA's inspection ID and carried out on one or more days. Inspections are ordered by their first day.
_Avoid_: visit, audit

**Report**:
The document HIQA publishes for one inspection. Each inspection has exactly one report.
_Avoid_: PDF

**Inspection kind**:
Whether an inspection judged regulations (a regulation inspection) or the National Standards (a thematic inspection).
_Avoid_: report kind, report type, inspection type (HIQA uses "type of inspection" for announced or unannounced)

**Regulation**:
A numbered provision HIQA judges against, either a care regulation ("Regulation 28: Fire precautions") or a registration regulation ("Registration Regulation 4"), identified by its family and number.
_Avoid_: rule, standard

**Judgment**:
HIQA's verdict on one regulation at one regulation inspection: Compliant, Substantially compliant or Not compliant.
_Avoid_: rating, score, grade

**Overall judgment**:
The single verdict of a thematic inspection against the National Standards. It is not a judgment and never produces a finding.
_Avoid_: thematic judgment, standards judgment

**Finding**:
A judgment of Substantially compliant or Not compliant. A Compliant judgment is not a finding.
_Avoid_: issue, non-compliance, violation

**Repeat finding**:
A finding that the inspector states repeats a finding from an earlier inspection. Only the inspector's statement makes a finding a repeat finding.
_Avoid_: recurring finding, persistent issue

**Evidence span**:
A verbatim passage from a published report, with its page, on which a statement in the record rests.
_Avoid_: finding sentence, quote, snippet, chunk

### Compliance plans

**Compliance plan**:
The registered provider's published response to the findings of one inspection: the provider's response per regulation, followed by HIQA's table of regulatory requirements. HIQA's own term. An inspection with no findings has none.
_Avoid_: action plan, improvement plan

**Regulatory requirement**:
One sub-provision of a regulation listed in a compliance plan, such as Regulation 28(2)(i), with its judgment, risk rating and compliance date. HIQA's own term.
_Avoid_: sub-regulation, clause

**Risk rating**:
HIQA's rating of a regulatory requirement: yellow (low), orange (moderate) or red (high).
_Avoid_: severity, priority

**Compliance date**:
HIQA's "date to be complied with" for a regulatory requirement. For a red rating the inspector sets it.
_Avoid_: deadline, due date

**Commitment**:
One action the provider states in its response, with its stated deadline.
_Avoid_: promise, promise item, action

**Stated deadline**:
When the provider says a commitment will be done, in the provider's own words ("week ending 25th July 2025", "Quarter 2 2026"). Shown alongside the compliance date, never merged with it.
_Avoid_: deadline, due date, target date

### Follow-up

**Re-assessment**:
What each later inspection of the same centre did with the regulation of an earlier finding: not re-assessed, or judged again as improved, unchanged or worsened. The first judgment again ends the re-assessment; if that judgment is itself a finding, following continues from it.
_Avoid_: follow-up state, fixed, resolved

**Follow-up statement**:
An evidence span in a later report that refers back to an earlier commitment, finding or compliance plan.
_Avoid_: follow-up, update, mention

**Reported outcome**:
What a follow-up statement says: reported done, reported in progress, reported not done, or unclear. A commitment with no follow-up statement shows "no statement found". "Reported not done" is only ever an inspector's words, never an inference.
_Avoid_: fixed, broken, kept, broken promise
