# Documentations

Everything the team produces outside the code: weekly meeting minutes, the weekly
presentation decks, and the design write-ups behind ClearCare features.

## Weekly files follow the sprint cadence

Meetings and presentations are **weekly files**, and the week number in their names
follows the **project sprint / project week cadence** — one meeting record and one
deck per sprint-week, numbered from Week 1 (the first team meeting, 18 September 2026).
Week numbers are therefore sprint numbers: Sprint 6 runs 16–23 October 2026 and its
files are `week_6.md` / `week-6.pptx`.

## Folders

| Path | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `meetings/` | Week-by-week minutes, `week_N.md` (attendance, agenda, decisions, actions) |
| `presentations/` | The weekly deck, `week_N.pptx`, one per sprint-week |
| `designs/` | Design and research write-ups, named by sprint or by task (`sprint-6-ui-changes.md`, `easier-to-read-toggle.md`) |

## Adding weekly files

1. Meeting minutes go in `meetings/` as `week_<N>.md`, using the same headings as the
   earlier weeks (project title, group members, date, time, location, attendance,
   agenda, ...).
2. The deck goes in `presentations/` as `week_<N>.pptx`.
3. `<N>` is the current project week / sprint number.

Longer design documents belong in `designs/` and should name the sprint they came from,
so the reasoning can be traced back to the week it was discussed.
