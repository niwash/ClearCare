# Phase 1 (Pre-public-launch) user evaluation

**Created:** 26 October 2026\
**Modified:** 28 October 2026

Informal feedback from participants before ClearCare's public launch. Each was asked what they liked, what they disliked, and what they would add. The site was changing quickly, so each participant's commit is listed to show which version they reviewed.

Some reviewers gave a general review rather than answering the three questions. These were also accepted.

## General User 1

### Likes

- It looks good, and it's a good start.
- The accessibility options, dark mode and Easier to read, are nice options for older people.

### Dislikes

- The fonts. There are lots of different font styles on the page.
- The ClearCare name in the header uses a different font in Easier to read mode. Unless that's intended, they would keep the same font and increase the font size instead.

### Suggestions

- None. They have no experience with care homes or services for older people, so weren't sure what to add. They felt the site already covers a lot.

## General User 2

### Likes

- It's very straightforward, and Easier to read is good functionality.
- They really liked "Check a nursing home's record with HIQA". Most families look at this first, because it's about the safety of their family member.

### Dislikes

- With Easier to read off, too much text appears at once. It feels overwhelming and cluttered, although it's very informative and helpful.
- They asked whether the text could be reduced so it's easier on the eyes but keeps the same information, without needing Easier to read mode.

### Suggestions

- "Add a way to check the staff at a nursing home: whether nurses and carers are qualified and able to care for older people. For example, by pulling in reviews or qualifications. Nothing like this currently exists in Ireland, so families have no way to be sure their relative is in good hands. This would give them that reassurance."

# General User 3

### Comments

- Went through all the sections and it is very simple and easy to navigate.
- Contents are very informative to their needs which is great help.
- Good tool to start for the family who doesn't have a clue where to start.
- Having HIQA results is a plus as well for them to define the best and appropriate home for their loved ones.

# General User 4

### Likes
- The details are complete, especially about Fair Deal because a lot of patients' families don't know about this.
- The website has statistics of the performance as well.
- Search engine and contact info.

### Suggestions
