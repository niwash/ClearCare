## 3. Semi-structured interview questions

### 1. What did you like about the website?

"It's good. I like the icons the colours, it's easy to read and understand."

### 2. What did you dislike about the website?

"I got a little bit confused on the 'not compliant' at the start. I was so close to searching what this [substantially compliant] mean. Does it mean not much or partial, little? What does it mean kinda thing. Another thing is the map, I found myself used to zooming in the map using my mouse, which kept scrolling the page up and down instead of actually zooming. So having to click the buttons instead of using the mouse scroll was new and slightly unfamiliar."

### 3. Is there something you would suggest/like to see?

"It's great for people that can speak English properly, but it might find it a little bit difficult for someone who doesn't have English as their first language."
