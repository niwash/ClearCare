## 3. Semi-structured interview questions

### 1. What did you like about the website?

"I really like this shortlist actually so that I can just click on compare, instead of going back and forth, or like what I usually do is opening a new tab. I like this because it's just a clear table, and I think it highlights the important things that most people wouldn't care about nursing homes like the rooms, types of care, activities, facilities, and regulation compliances. You can also go back to the page to check more about it. I really like the fair deal page."

### 2. What did you dislike about the website?

None recorded.

### 3. Is there something you would suggest/like to see?

"I feel like the fair deal page, I don't know how, but I think it should be more eye-catching. Because when I was starting to search, I didn't pay attention to it and even when I was reading the nursing home details, even though it mentioned fair deal. It didn't really come across my mind to check what that is. I'm not exactly sure how, but considering that it's very useful, it should catch more attention."

"I wouldn't have noticed right away if you didn't ask me about it, but the more I explore the website, the more likely I'd notice it."

"Some people aren't particularly good with reading so maybe an overview popping up when hovering over something?"

## Interviewer notes

- Participant demonstrated a higher level of digital literacy in comparison to past participants. They navigated the website with confidence and made fluent use of trackpad gestures.
- Participant effectively used the search filter based on their preferences, noting down how few results are available when choosing certain amenities.
- Participant fully read the Fair Deal page.

## Possible tickets

- "None found in the report" must be reworded to "None found in the latest inspection".
- Make Fair Deal stand out more.
- Make the Easier to Read and dark mode toggles easier to find (e.g., an accessibility option).
- Reconsider the placement or wording of the 999 and 112 emergency line on Support Services.
- Explain the price terms (maximum contract price, full weekly cost) on the centre and comparison pages.
- Show a short explanation when hovering over unfamiliar terms.
