## 3. Semi-structured interview questions

### 1. How did you find the website?

[Participant goes back to home]. "What I liked about the website was that it's very clear, and it doesn't look tacky or ugly. I think, sometimes if you just go into a search bar and Google something, you might not necessarily trust the website. I would trust [this] with the layout, especially since you can scroll and find things very easily. Very easy."

"I think the searching and comparing homes is quite a good thing as well to have, especially since you don't have to open multiple tabs. This website's comparison feature is much easier to find in comparison to [example website mentioned]. Fair Deal, for sure I would've come across that and looked into it because now I'm just going through everything. I liked the pictograms, the link to the websites, the emails and the phone numbers are very clearly marked. Sometimes it might be a little bit busy, I'm not sure if that's necessarily good or bad. For example [in resources], there's two big bubbles on top of the three small bubbles. The need to talk to someone banner, you might just scroll past it, I think I might just skim over it. I liked the layout [official information section]."

[Founds National Figures]. "I don't really know what to think of this page, maybe it's just something I'm not really thinking about. Why is that [4 in 10 bubble] a different size? I suppose that being at the end of the website [navigation bar] isn't that bad, because I don't think it's necessary something people care about too much, but there's always some statistic-head, there's always someone who really enjoys that. So not bad to have it. The ordering [of the navigation bar] is really good."

### 2. Is there something you would suggest/like to see?

"Usually on a website I'd expect to see a contact us. I know you're not necessarily like a company, but usually I'd email the company if I have a comment. It also makes it more human. This [the search bar on the home] might need to stand out a little bit more. I know if my mom is on it, she would be like 'where would I look'. Especially rip.ie there would be a search bar on the top and on the bottom."
