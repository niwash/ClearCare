## 3. Semi-structured interview questions

### 1. What did you like about the website?

"Visually it is appealing, especially with easier to read and dark mode, I think that's a really cool feature especially with the easier to read, there's a bit indicator of the mouse that would help older people looking through the page. I also liked how smooth it functions [the map], it doesn't glitch. I also like how modern it looks, it's not boring to look at. Some websites look outdated, gloomy and not appealing. I especially liked the map feature, I love a functioning map. So, it's easy for people to look at certain places [participant searched with an Eircode and filtered by county and it worked]. It does not look like a gimmick. I liked the graph, you can see the previous years."

### 2. What did you dislike about the website?

"When it came to the inspection history tab, it 'irked' me a bit. Visually it's appealing, but I also do think you could do the wording a bit better. Like 'substantially compliant', I don't like that word. I feel like a better word could be there. 'Partial' or 'Partially compliant' would be nicer."

"I'm a bit confused with what to look at first and what happened next tabs, the wording. To be honest you did put something under what happened next, which is fine, but I wouldn't know initially what it is. It doesn't make sense to me, it made me confused a bit. It's a bit misleading, and I'm curious as to why it was put there. I think it's preferable if you changed the title of the tab."

### 3. Is there something you would suggest/like to see?

"This might just be me being nitpicky, but these two functions [easier to read and dark mode] shouldn't be there. I think it should be separated from somewhere else, but having it beside the shortlist and login, it doesn't seem like it fits there. Maybe an accessibility option?"
