# Thematic reports are read from their cover and kept apart from regulation inspections

A thematic inspection judges a centre against the National Standards and ends with one overall judgment, where a regulation inspection judges regulations. Thematic reports now have the mapping of their own that ADR-0013 expected: `parser/thematic_mapper.csv` and `parser/thematic_fields.csv`. The ingest tries that mapping first. Its first section starts at the title that only a thematic report has, so any other report goes on to the regulation parse. The mapping takes the Centre ID, the type of inspection, the date of inspection and the Fieldwork ID from the cover. The overall judgment has no label. It is the first cell of the one table row under the "Overall Judgment" heading that has two cells, and the second cell is HIQA's description of it. Each report becomes a row of `clearcare.thematic_report` (db/V23), written in the same batch as the regulation reports. The overall judgment is stored as the report prints it, and we keep no list of the values it may have. A thematic report that is read counts as loaded. One with a value missing counts as failed and is tried again on the next run. The cover also names the provider, who can be a person, so the mapping has no row for that name and it is not stored.

Thematic reports could have been rows of `inspection_report` with no regulations. The re-assessment views (ADR-0015, ADR-0024) take a centre's next row in that table as its next report, so those views and every later view over the table would have to leave thematic reports out. We could also have read the body of the report. It describes what the inspector saw, and a family needs nothing from it beyond the overall judgment.

A thematic inspection is therefore never a centre's next report, and an overall judgment never produces a finding. Nothing reads the table yet. The API gets a view when it first serves thematic inspections. A change to the layout of thematic reports is an edit to their mapping. Of the 232 thematic reports held on 12 November 2026, 230 are read. One of the other two prints its date of inspection as "01May 2025" and one prints a Centre ID without "OSV-", so they still fail. The layouts the ingest could not read before, other than thematic reports, still count as failed on every run (ADR-0022).
