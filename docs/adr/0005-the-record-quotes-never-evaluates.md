# The record quotes; it never evaluates

Every statement in the record is the regulator's or the provider's own published words, with its date and page. ClearCare does not rank, score or recommend centres, and does not write or suggest complaints. A commitment is shown as not done only when an inspector says so; the absence of a statement is shown as absence, never as failure. Dates are kept as written, and when two sources disagree both are shown. Named individuals (persons in charge, management contacts, residents) are not recorded, because the PSI licence under which HIQA's reports are reused excludes personal data.

A future contributor will be tempted to add a score, a traffic light or a "fixed" flag. This decision is the reason not to: the record's value is that every claim can be traced to someone else's published words.
