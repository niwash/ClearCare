# ClearCare – Self-Service User Stories

## User Story 1: Finding the Right Care for Myself

### Who is the user?

Michael is 68 and lives alone in Dublin. He has always been independent and has managed well at home, but over the past year he has started to realise that he may eventually need more support with everyday activities. His family has offered to help him look at nursing homes, but Michael wants to be involved in the decision himself rather than having someone else choose for him.

### Why is he looking?

Michael decides to start researching his options early. He wants to know what homes are available in areas he would be comfortable living in and, more importantly, what life and care might actually be like there.

He isn't ready to move immediately, but he wants to understand his options before he reaches a point where he has to make a decision quickly.

### What does he find difficult?

While searching online, Michael comes across several nursing homes but finds it difficult to work out how they compare. He discovers that HIQA publishes inspection reports, but the reports are lengthy and contain a lot of information that he doesn't immediately understand.

He is particularly interested in knowing whether a home has had problems in areas such as residents' rights, staffing, care planning or safety, and whether those problems appeared again during later inspections.

### What does Michael want?

Michael doesn't want a website to simply tell him which nursing home is "best". He wants to see the evidence and make that judgement for himself.

He wants to be able to understand a home's inspection history, identify important findings and see how those findings changed over time.

### How does ClearCare help?

With ClearCare, Michael can look at an individual nursing home and follow its inspection history over time. Instead of having to search through several separate reports, he can see the relevant inspection findings in one place and trace them back to the original HIQA report.

This gives him a clearer picture of the home's history and helps him decide which homes he wants to contact or visit.

### The outcome

Michael uses ClearCare not to make the decision for him, but to help him understand the information well enough to make his own decision about his future care.

### User story

> As someone considering a nursing home for myself, I want to understand a home's inspection history and the evidence behind its findings, so that I can make an informed decision about my own future care.

## User Story 2: Taking Control of My Next Move

### Who is the user?

David is 76 and has been living in his own apartment in Dublin for many years. Recently, he has found that managing everything by himself is becoming more difficult. His daughter has suggested that he consider moving into a nursing home, but David doesn't want the decision to be made entirely by his family.

He wants to take the time to explore the options himself and find somewhere where he feels comfortable and well cared for.

### What starts his search?

David's main concern is maintaining his independence for as long as possible. He isn't simply looking for somewhere close to his family. He wants to understand what different homes actually offer and whether they have a good record of caring for their residents.

He starts looking online and creates a list of a few homes that seem suitable.

### What does he struggle with?

Once he starts researching the homes, David discovers the HIQA inspection reports. He likes the fact that they provide information about what inspectors actually observed, but he finds it difficult to make sense of several reports for different homes.

Some reports mention issues that were later addressed, while others contain findings that he isn't sure how to interpret. David doesn't want to judge a home based on one negative statement without understanding the wider context.

### What does he want to know?

David wants to understand what has actually happened at each home over time.

He wants to know:

- What did inspectors find?
- Which areas were compliant or not compliant?
- Were issues identified again during later inspections?
- Has the home improved in areas where problems were previously identified?
- Where can he find the original evidence?

### How does ClearCare fit into his decision?

David uses ClearCare to explore the inspection history of the homes on his shortlist. Rather than opening several long PDF reports and trying to remember what each one said, he can follow the findings across inspections and go back to the original HIQA evidence when something catches his attention.

This doesn't give David a recommendation about where he should live. Instead, it gives him the information he needs to ask better questions, decide which homes he wants to visit and ultimately make the decision himself.

### The outcome

After researching several homes, David feels much more prepared to speak with his family and contact the homes directly. He still wants to visit them before making a decision, but he now has a clearer understanding of their inspection histories and knows what questions he wants to ask.

### User story

> As someone looking for a nursing home for myself, I want to understand what inspectors have found about different homes over time, so that I can decide which homes I want to consider for my own care.
