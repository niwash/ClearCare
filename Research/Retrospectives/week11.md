# Week 11 Retrospective

## What went well this week

The landmarking and the interviews are now done. With both pieces of research finished, everyone can contribute equally to development and the gold set. The phase 2 interviews gave us a lot of insight into the website. The alerts were finally completed, and the map and the user experience were improved.

## What didn’t go well this week

Some pages of the website, such as the guides, have not been monitored or evaluated, so their information has inaccuracies and their UX/UI has barely improved. Too much time was spent on research that turned out not to be useful for the website. We also need to look into the colour palette of the website, especially for the easier to read mode. The website performance testing and benchmarking, and the testing on the production clusters, did not go as planned.

## What can we improve on

We should check every page of the website to make sure it is up to date. As we are in the last few weeks, we should focus on the remaining core features and the most important changes, and keep polishing the website. We also need to address the feedback from the mentors and close the open issues in the repository.

## What we will do next week

Next week, the team will address the feedback and continue the phase 2 evaluation, complete the gold set and its evaluation, continue testing, start work on the final report, and get more involved in development.
