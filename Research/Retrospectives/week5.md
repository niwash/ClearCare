# Week 5 Retrospective

## What went well

Week 5 showed progress across development, data processing, and deployment. The team began work on amenities extraction from HIQA PDFs, and the parsing work was completed. Automated deployments and Docker Compose were also set up, allowing team members to deploy and test the application locally. We made progress on the pipeline and added more information, including guides, to the production site. With parsing available, the site can now display the regulations for each loaded report.

## What went wrong

Although the sprint made good progress, some concerns and criticisms from the previous presentation were not tracked in the project management system, so they were only addressed at the last minute or remained unresolved. Development was slower than expected, partly because infrastructure work took time. We also could have prepared better to explain the technical complexities during the presentation. An initial user evaluation did not take place, and tasks such as the Fair Deal Guide and research into adult-centred user design took longer than expected.

## What we will add/improve

The team agreed to address the outstanding concerns and backlog tasks. These items need to be tracked clearly so they can be prioritised and followed through rather than left until the last minute.

## What we could do better

We need to track feedback and criticism from presentations more consistently, make sure tasks are recorded in project management, and complete work on time. The team also identified a need to increase development speed and progress on data analysis. Better time management and clearer prioritisation should help us address concerns earlier and avoid tasks spreading beyond their expected timelines.
