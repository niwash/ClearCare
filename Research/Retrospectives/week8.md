# Week 8 Retrospective

## What went well this week

This week was one of the most collaborative the team has had, with a lot of individual and shared progress and many features added to the site. The amenities can now be displayed on the website, and decisions and changes on the amenities were made quickly despite the circumstances. The migration finally finished, and user accounts and preferences were completed, which together removed the main blockers for other work.

## What didn’t go well this week

The scheduling for the amenities was not completed, so work remains there that could have been finished this week. Although many features were added to the site, their design was not considered properly and was only looked over at the last minute, and other last-minute changes were also needed. We also felt that the site's design is being developed from a developer’s perspective rather than from the perspective of the target audience.

## What can we improve on

The team agreed to focus more on the user’s perspective. Putting ourselves in the audience’s shoes should give us more insight into how to design the site, rather than designing it the way we would like it ourselves. To support this, we should reach out to relevant external groups and users for their opinions and accelerate the research progress. We also need to handle edge cases in our data better.

## What we will do next week

Next week, the team will complete the amenities scheduling and data evaluation, and carry out more user-side research, possibly including visits to relevant organisations. UI development will continue for the new features, and we will investigate UX/UI design in preparation for Phase 2 of the user evaluation. We will also work on the AI chatbot, and on pictures and alerts for the site.
