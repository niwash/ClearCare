# Week 7 Retrospective

## What went well this week

This week, the team made good progress across several areas. The migration to AWS went well, and the website was updated and is looking sharper. We also received a few responses for the Phase 1 user evaluations and got the first version of the comparison grid underway. Collaboration within the team was positive, and good progress was made on the LLM extraction, although further changes are still needed.

## What didn’t go well this week

There were some personal commitments that affected progress during the week. The public release for Phase 2 could not happen as planned because additional time was needed to research the legalities. We also recognised that the AWS migration could have been carried out earlier, as its importance was not identified soon enough in the development process. In addition, the LLM extraction was not completed as hoped, which delayed the move to other data-related tasks.

## What can we improve on

The team identified a few areas for improvement. Better personal time management would help maintain progress, while outreach for user evaluations should continue. We should also research other websites for design ideas and investigate competitors. Development could be accelerated by working on tasks in parallel, and tasks should be divided more evenly throughout the week.

## What we will do next week

Next week, the team will focus on finalising user accounts and migrating the pipelines on AWS. Work will continue on improving the website design, with further outreach for Phase 2 evaluations and the possibility of starting Phase 3. We will also work on the interactive map and geocoding. On the data side, the team plans to begin data analysis and ontology research, finish the amenities work, and start working on the research questions and Gold Set version 2.
