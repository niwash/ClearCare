# Week 3 Retrospective

## What Went Well

The team felt that Week 3 was productive in several areas. The
interviews provided more insight into real-world data and helped improve
our understanding of the problem we are trying to solve. The HIQA report
audits also contributed to our research by helping us identify relevant
information and patterns in inspection reports. Additionally, we
received data from our mentor, which supported our ongoing analysis.
Another positive development was that we were able to deploy a prototype
of our user interface, allowing us to make progress towards
demonstrating the ClearCare concept.

## What Did Not Go Well

Although we made progress, the team identified several areas that needed
improvement. We had not received a response from Sage, which limited
progress in that area. I also felt that we had not made sufficient
progress with the development side of the project. Furthermore, we had
not completed end-user interviews, meaning we still needed more direct
feedback from potential users. We also recognised that we could have
started compiling the nursing home registration list earlier to avoid
delays in that part of the work.

## How We Can Improve

The team agreed that better time management and task planning would help
us work more effectively. We felt that setting more deadlines and
dividing larger tasks into daily goals would make progress easier to
track and reduce the risk of work being delayed. We also recognised the
importance of encouraging every team member to contribute to the
codebase so that development responsibilities are shared. Finally, we
need to gather stronger evidence about end-user needs, particularly
through interviews and other relevant research, to ensure that ClearCare
is based on genuine user requirements.
