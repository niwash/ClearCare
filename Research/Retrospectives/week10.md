# Week 10 Retrospective

## What went well this week

The end-user interviews were the highlight of the week. Four interviews were carried out, all recorded with consent, which gave us a comprehensive analysis for the user evaluation. For the first time, changes to the site were driven by real user input instead of our own judgement, and most of the feedback has already been applied. There was also high individual contribution across the team, and the alerts work showed that alerts could easily be extended beyond report ingestion.

## What didn’t go well this week

There was less collaboration this week, as most of the work was individual and there were few shared tasks. Some team members were held back by personal commitments. The alerts were not completed, and the gold set is still pending, so some tasks are running slightly behind.

## What can we improve on

Team members should give each other more detailed updates on their individual tasks, since we only had a general idea of what others were working on. We still need more user feedback, but we do not need to act on every suggestion. As we near the end of the module, we should spend more time polishing the website and increase collaboration and activity over the final weeks.

## What we will do next week

Next week, the team will continue the UX/UI evaluation, finalise the Fair Deal Calculator, the gold set and evaluation, and the legality documents, document data cleaning and competing websites, benchmark the website, and start drafting the report.
