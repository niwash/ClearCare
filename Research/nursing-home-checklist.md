# Are You Ready to Search for a Nursing Home?

Before you start browsing homes, it helps to pause and ask yourself a few key questions. This checklist walks you through the stages most families go through, so you can search with clarity and confidence.

---

## Stage 1: Recognising the Need

- [ ] Have you accepted that a nursing home is the right next step, rather than home care or another option?
- [ ] Do you know whose decision this is, and is the person involved in making it?
- [ ] Has a HSE care needs assessment been done, or do you know how to get one?

---

## Stage 2: Understanding the Care Required

- [ ] Do you know what level of care is needed, whether that is personal care, nursing care, or specialist dementia care?
- [ ] Do you know whether those needs are likely to change, and have you thought about a home that can grow with them?

---

## Stage 3: Sorting Out Funding

- [ ] Do you know about the Fair Deal scheme and whether you qualify?
- [ ] Do you know what the application process involves and how long it takes?
- [ ] Do you understand what costs Fair Deal does not cover?

---

## Stage 4: Knowing What You Want

- [ ] Do you know which area or county you want to search in?
- [ ] Have you thought about how often family will visit and how far is realistic?
- [ ] Do you know what amenities matter most to the person moving in?
- [ ] Have you thought about their daily routine, social preferences, and any cultural, religious, or language needs?

---

## Stage 5: Evaluating Homes

- [ ] Do you know what HIQA is and how to read an inspection report?
- [ ] Do you know what questions to ask when you visit a home?
- [ ] Do you know what to look for in a contract before signing?
